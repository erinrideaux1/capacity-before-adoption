# Diagnostic Framework

1. Unscheduled time is not synonymous with available capacity.
2. Fragmentation affects the usability of nominally open time.
3. Back-to-back meetings are evidence about operating conditions, not meeting value.
4. Calendar patterns should surface questions rather than infer motivation.
5. New organizational expectations require consideration of existing workload and capacity.
6. Calendar data alone cannot establish AI adoption, leader effectiveness, or workload.
7. Human review is required before recommending what should be eliminated, delegated, shortened, consolidated, or redesigned.

**Core principle:** Calendar patterns are evidence for discovery—not evidence for judgment.
